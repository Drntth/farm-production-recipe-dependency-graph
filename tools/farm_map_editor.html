Farm Map Editor

Load the stitched screenshot of the empty farm (PNG). Everything stays in this browser; nothing is uploaded.
Zoom: mouse wheel, touchpad pinch or + / -. Pan: left drag (Calibrate tab), right or middle drag, Space + drag or the arrow keys. 0: fit.
Ruler: two rows of fields in an L shape from one corner field, one row along ↘ and one along ↙. Click the three points (zoom in for precision):
  1. A: top corner of the corner field
  2. B: right corner of the last field of the ↘ row
  3. C: left corner of the last field of the ↙ row
Not calibrated.
Fixed buildings (farmhouse, mine, truck, …). Pick an id, then drag over every tile it blocks (e.g. the truck with its order board). Dragging again moves it. Barn and silo are optional: without a position the layout planner places them.
    Overlay another image (e.g. the wiki plot map) to trace plot borders that are gone from the screenshot. Load it, then click 3 points spread far apart, each first on the overlay (r) and then on the same spot of the farm (m): r1, m1, r2, m2, r3, m3. Lower the opacity to see the farm under it. A left drag pans. The overlay is not saved in the JSON.
    No overlay.
    Farm plots (wiki: Expansion/Farm). Add a plot, then drag rectangles to cover its tiles; touching rectangles merge into one shape. Alt + drag erases tiles of the selected plot. Draw the starting land as section base.
      Mark tiles by hand. Buildable (green): the farm sections; the layout stays inside them. Blocked (red): water, road, forest, … never used. Add a zone, then drag rectangles; touching rectangles merge. Alt + drag erases tiles of the selected zone.
        Relative to config/farm_map.json; save the screenshot as config/farm_background.png.
